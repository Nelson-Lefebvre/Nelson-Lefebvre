## Featured project

### [Alberta Energy Operations Intelligence](https://github.com/Nelson-Lefebvre/alberta-energy-bi)

Public Alberta oil and gas data (Petrinex production filings, the AER well register, WCS and gas prices; 4.34M fact rows),
ingested with Python into **Databricks** (Delta Lake, Unity Catalog), modelled with **dbt** into a star schema, refreshed by
a monthly job and reported in **Power BI** (27 DAX measures, row-level security). Production, prices and wells are real;
costs are simulated.

Two errors passed every structural test: a gas unit mismatch put one region's OPEX/boe at $4, and 11,943 wells carried
CO₂ with no production underneath. Grain-level plausibility tests now catch both.
